# Lab 8: Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging

## Overview

In this lab, I used **NLTK** and **spaCy** to identify the parts of speech of words in different sentences. I explored **coarse-grained** and **fine-grained POS tags** to understand how words are classified based on their grammatical roles.

## What I Did

- Used **spaCy** to identify and explain the POS tags for the word **"play"** and the other words in a sentence.
- Compared how **"Google"** and **"table"** can be used as either nouns or verbs depending on the context.
- Used **displaCy** to visualize the grammatical relationships between words in a sentence.

## What I Learned

This lab helped me understand how the grammatical role of a word can change depending on its context and how NLP tools can be used to analyze sentence structure.
